Our story

From one bench to a regional reference lab.

Nexora Labs began in 1999 with a single chemistry analyser, two technologists, and a conviction: patients deserve results that are fast, accurate, and explained with care. That conviction still guides everything we do.

Today our campus houses a full-service clinical laboratory, a specialised microbiology wing, and a digital pathology suite. Yet every specimen is still treated the way the very first one was — with meticulous attention and respect for the person behind the label.

  • CLIA-certified and CAP-accredited since 2003
  • Board-certified pathologists review every report
  • Fully automated pre-analytics with barcode tracking
  • Dedicated courier network across five counties
  • Secure physician and patient web portals
