About Nexora Labs

Accuracy you can trust.

Founded in 1999, Nexora Labs has grown from a small clinical lab into a regional reference laboratory serving over 200 healthcare providers. We combine state-of-the-art instrumentation with rigorous quality control to deliver results clinicians can rely on.

Our team of board-certified pathologists, microbiologists, and lab scientists processes thousands of specimens every month — each one handled with the same care and precision as the first.

  • CLIA-certified and CAP-accredited laboratory
  • Same-day results on most routine panels
  • Board-certified pathologists on every report
  • Secure online portal for physicians and patients
  • Free courier pickup for clinic partners
